How to play

You start with a king on e1, layer 5 and 64 pawns. Eight pawns fill a2 through h2 on each layer. Each layer starts with a full opposing army.

Taking a turn

In 3D, select a piece to highlight its legal destinations across all eight layers. Tilt or zoom the camera to get a clear view, then select a destination to move. In 2D, change layers with the numbered buttons, the cube, or the 1–8 keys. Your selection stays active when you change views or layers.

After your move, the armies on your king’s layer and your piece’s destination layer reply together. A Voodoo Knight hop also activates the army on its destination layer. Stockfish plays on your king’s layer, and a tactical bot plays on the other active layers. Each army stays on its layer and normally makes one reply.

Cross-layer attacks

Amber markers show squares your pieces attack from another layer. Sliding attacks extend through kings. Sliding pieces need a clear path to move.

A cross-layer check activates the threatened army on any layer. If a safe king move exists, the king takes it. The army can make extra moves to open and use a safe escape in the same turn. If no escape opens, the king stays in check.

Your pieces

King
Moves to any of the 26 adjacent cells.
Pawn
Advances one rank, or two on its first move if clear. Captures one rank forward with a one-cell shift in file, layer, or both. En passant follows the same geometry after an enemy double step. Promote on rank 8.
Knight
Jumps two cells along one axis and one along a perpendicular axis.
Bishop
Slides along two-axis diagonals in any plane.
Rook
Slides straight up or down through layers.
Trishop
Slides along three-axis space diagonals.
Queen
Slides along a rank or file, straight up or down through layers, or along any two-axis diagonal.

Voodoo Knight

The first opposing army reduced to its king and one other piece starts a 10-player-turn countdown. When the countdown ends or that army is stalemated or checkmated, the Voodoo Knight spawns on an empty square outside enemy attack on that layer. If there is no safe square, it waits on standby for the same layer and tries again after the board changes.

The Knight waits one player turn per remaining opposing army between hop attempts. For example, with five armies left, it tries to hop every five turns. At each scheduled hop, the Knight randomly chooses a safe 3D knight move before the bots reply. It can capture an undefended piece if its landing square is safe after the capture and any pawn succession. If there is no safe move, it stays put until the next scheduled hop. Your king perishes if the Voodoo Knight is captured.

Winning and losing

Clear all eight layers to win. Each time you capture a king, an unpromoted pawn on that layer is crowned if one remains. Safe pawns are chosen first. Capturing a king with no pawns left clears the layer.

You lose if your king is in check and every move, including escapes across layers, leaves it in check.
